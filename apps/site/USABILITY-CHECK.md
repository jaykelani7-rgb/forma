# First-visit usability check

This is a protocol for real participant sessions, not a completed research report. Automated browser checks cannot establish whether first-time visitors understand the product.

Use five people unfamiliar with Forma. Ask each to think aloud without explaining the interface first. Use demo content only; do not ask for credentials or personal notebook data.

1. After viewing the opening screen, ask: “What do you think Forma helps you do?” Record their own words.
   Let them choose their first action without prompting. Record whether they explore, scroll, leave, or hesitate; do not assume a calmer layout improves retention without observing this. Then ask them to open the interactive story, keep a thought, fold the Ribbon, and return to it. Check whether they recognize the same code and words in the companion. Observe whether Play or the manual steps are easier to discover on their device, and whether closing and reopening the story preserves their sense of place.
2. Ask them to try the sample problem and keep a thought. Record where they hesitate or need help.
3. Ask them to save a reflection and find it in the workspace Journal. Check whether they recognize their own words.
4. Ask them to reopen that problem. Check whether they expect their earlier code and note to remain.
5. Ask them to finish recall and find how to get Forma for Mac. Check whether they distinguish the prepared Tutor sample from a live AI session.

6. Ask them to choose an active track and find its connection to Today. Check whether they distinguish an independent learning outcome from platform acceptance.
7. Ask them to keep a contest takeaway, queue an upsolve, and return after practice. Check whether they understand why the original contest result stays unchanged.

For each task, record completion without help, points of confusion, and the participant’s expectation versus the actual result. Do not infer success from speed alone. Prioritize repeated failures before adding further visual effects. Obtain consent before recording a session.
